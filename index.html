  • File Too Small (Under 10KB): A heavily cropped black-and-white signature can sometimes compress down to 6KB or 8KB, causing a "file size should be between 10 to 20 KB" error. Selecting our 10 – 20 KB preset paired with 200 × 80 px (4.0 × 2.0 cm) keeps the output cleanly around 14KB–19KB.

  • Blurry or Dark Background: Sign with a dark black or blue pen on unruled white paper in bright daylight, crop tightly around the signature, and then run it through our 10 – 20 KB preset.
